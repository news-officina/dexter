Moscow bars Europe from US-mediated Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi

Kushner and Witkoff mediate the first direct negotiations since February, as EU leaders decide this week who speaks for Europe. European security advisers remain outside the room.

10 November 2026

The first US-mediated talks between Ukraine and Russia since February opened in Abu Dhabi, with the US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff mediating for Washington. Moscow barred European officials from the main negotiations. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said the talks would run "between Russia and Ukraine, with mediation by the United States." Security advisers from France, Germany and the United Kingdom remain present but outside the room.

Day 1 will take up security guarantees for Ukraine and the reshaping of Europe's security architecture; Day 2 will take up territory, according to Politico and the Kyiv Post. The French presidency said there can be no durable settlement in Ukraine without taking European security into account, and no agreement on security in Europe without the Europeans. EU leaders will now decide who speaks for Europe at an informal summit in Dublin later this week.

"They will not solve the Donetsk issue and the territorial issue until they know what the security architecture is going to be after the war," a senior US official said. Reuters reported on November 10, citing a US official, that both sides have shown private flexibility on a possible territorial exchange.

Moscow demands that Kyiv recognise Russian control over Crimea and the four regions it claims to have annexed: Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson. Moscow also demands that Kyiv withdraw its forces from all four, including areas Russia has never occupied. Kyiv rejects those demands. Zelenskyy said Ukraine's team would propose de-escalatory steps in Abu Dhabi and seek a trilateral meeting at the leaders' level to end the war.

A senior US official said before the talks began that in order to end the war, Europe must be at the table. Russia and Ukraine used the days before Abu Dhabi to step up attacks, with Russia striking Ukrainian energy infrastructure as fighting continued across several regions.
