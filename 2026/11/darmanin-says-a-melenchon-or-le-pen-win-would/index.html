Darmanin says a Mélenchon or Le Pen win would end the EU as it exists today

He warned both programmes are built on the end of this Europe and urged a pro-European candidate to use the presidency to turn Europe into a power.

3 November 2026

Gérald Darmanin warned in Barcelona that a victory by Jean-Luc Mélenchon or Marine Le Pen in the presidential election would mean the end of the European Union as it exists today. There would be a continent and countries talking to each other, but no European Community. The next French president, he said, must turn Europe into a Europe of power, not a Europe of discussion.

Speaking at the forum in Barcelona, Darmanin said the programmes of both candidates are built on the end of this Europe. He argued that Europe is falling behind in the world, and that France tends to fall behind within it.

Darmanin's demand was specific: a very pro-European candidate must win the first round, set for 18 April, and use the presidency to turn Europe into a power that can act.

The task, as Darmanin defined it, is a Europe of power, not a Europe of discussion.

Darmanin said French debates are often what he called old people's debates, about pensions and health insurance.

The world, he told the forum, talks about quantum physics, artificial intelligence, climate and green industry.

On 15 October 2026, the National Assembly adopted France's 30.9 billion euro contribution to the EU budget for 2027, against opposition from LFI and the RN. The RN demanded the deepest cut and voted against it. Marine Le Pen left her seat empty and travelled to Brussels instead, the day before the summit on the next EU budget.

Darmanin once again placed the two extremes in the same basket, this time on their vision of Europe, as 20 Minutes noted in its heading. The outlet presented the phrase without his condition of election.
