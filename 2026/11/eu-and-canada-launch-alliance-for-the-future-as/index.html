EU and Canada launch Alliance for the Future as Canada joins EU defence procurement scheme

Canada becomes the first non-European participant in the EU's Safe scheme and has won contracts to supply Poland and Latvia, though the EU stopped short of the associate membership Von der Leyen had promised.

9 November 2026

The EU and Canada launched an Alliance for the Future at the 21st EU-Canada summit, including cooperation on security, the Council said. Canada is the first non-European participant in Security Action for Europe (Safe), the EU's defence procurement scheme, and its firms have won contracts to supply radios to Poland and helmets to Latvia. An EU spokesperson said the model would be open to other close and trusted partners.

Von der Leyen had promised Canada associate membership, and Canadian prime minister Mark Carney said in Montreal, "Today we move toward associate membership for Canada"; the EU offered an ever-closer association instead. Germany, backed by Greece and others, had objected in September to the term, citing legal uncertainty and the expectations it might raise elsewhere. Trump threatened tariffs on Europe if he judged the arrangement a "hostile act".

Carney has proposed a Defence, Security and Resilience Bank, an international financial institution that would pool funds from allies to offer loans for defence procurement. The EU-Canada joint statement merely takes note of that proposal.

Philipp Lausberg, a senior policy analyst at the European Policy Centre, said existing trade agreements and partnerships are ill-equipped to deal with weaponised trade, supply-chain disruptions, economic coercion and strategic dependencies. The EU's cooperation with partners rests, in his words, on assumptions of a world that does not exist anymore. He argued the Canada partnership could form a blueprint for deeper ties with Japan, South Korea and Australia.

Lausberg said there is a narrow window: a concrete roadmap must be ready by the June 2027 summit, ideally before France's presidential election in April and May. An EU official said other countries have already expressed interest in the alliance.
