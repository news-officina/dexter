Europeans shut out of Ukraine-Russia talks as US brokers Abu Dhabi negotiations

Russian and Ukrainian teams held a second day of Kushner-brokered talks while European delegates held separate consultations, with Lavrov rejecting a ceasefire. Moscow wants Ukraine to cede roughly 2,000 square miles of Donetsk and Luhansk it failed to conquer.

11 November 2026

Ukraine and Russia held a second straight day of US-brokered talks in Abu Dhabi on 11 November. The talks resumed face-to-face on 10 November after nine months, brokered by Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff. European officials flew in and were shut out of the trilateral room, holding separate consultations instead. Sergei Lavrov told them: "Well, they flew to Abu Dhabi. Let them sit there in the hotel."

According to Euronews and the Telegraph, Washington has signalled that they could eventually join the trilateral negotiations despite being shut out. Ukraine's president, Volodymyr Zelenskyy, called the presence of European representatives important and wants a common position of the EU, the United States and Ukraine.

Earlier on Tuesday, Ukrainian and American teams held separate discussions in Abu Dhabi with a European delegation. It included Jonathan Powell, Britain's national security adviser, and representatives from the US, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Nato, the European Commission and the European Council. Russia's Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov rejected a ceasefire, accusing Europeans of seeking a truce to deploy troops to Ukraine.

Territory reached the table. Zelenskyy said the Russians raised the issue of territories in Abu Dhabi. This, he said, is a matter for the leaders, and the negotiators have a mandate for it. A source close to the talks said the delegation never discussed concessions on occupied land and will never recognise it, neither de facto nor de jure.

Russia demands Ukraine cede roughly 2,000 square miles in Donetsk and Luhansk that Moscow's army failed to conquer, the same two outlets report. A source cited by TASS said Witkoff and Kushner may travel to Moscow within two weeks.
