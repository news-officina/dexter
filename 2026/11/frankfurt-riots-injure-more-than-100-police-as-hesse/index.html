Frankfurt riots injure more than 100 police as Hesse promises firmer response

Interior Minister Poseck did not rule out banning further assemblies after the looting of shops on the Zeil, while Mayor Josef refused his account of the suspects' origins.

1 November 2026

Hesse's interior minister has promised more stringent police action after Halloween-night riots in Frankfurt left more than 100 officers injured and shops looted. At a press conference on Sunday, he said the Zeil must not be left to a violent mob and did not rule out banning further assemblies. One officer was reported seriously hurt.

The afternoon demonstration was largely peaceful. Tagesschau reports that around 8,000 people demonstrated, organisers said 10,000 on Instagram. According to Tagesschau, police dissolved the gathering around 6:20 p.m. after pyrotechnics were set off and stones thrown at officers. What followed, according to Osthessen-News, took water cannons, a helicopter, tear gas and the special operations unit to contain. Police brought the situation under control only after midnight.

Police cleared and cordoned off the Hauptwache, the Konstablerwache and the Zeil on Saturday evening. The Zeil was sealed between the two squares with police vehicles, before events shifted towards Eschenheimer Tor and the Freßgass. The looting reached a running shop near the Konstablerwache. Around 22:58 an alarm went off at Jost Wiebelhaus's shop. Looters were inside and had shattered the 7.5 by 2 metre front window.

Interior Minister Poseck told reporters the arrested suspects were mostly German citizens aged 13 to 29. He described the roughly 1,000 to 1,500 troublemakers as predominantly "migrantisch geprägt". Mayor Mike Josef answered that he was "fassungslos und wütend", and refused to follow him on origin. "Mir ist völlig egal, welchen Pass jemand hat oder wo seine Eltern geboren sind", Josef said.

Minister President Boris Rhein said he was shocked by the violence and announced consequences. "Wer sich so verhält wie die Angreifer in der Frankfurter Innenstadt, stellt sich außerhalb unserer Gesellschaft", he said. The police admitted their own misjudgement of the situation. Looters attacked a weapons shop in the city centre; police secured two weapons and arrested several people, then a special operations unit secured the shop.
