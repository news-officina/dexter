EU drops associate membership label for Canada after German objection

The 28 October statement instead offers 'ever closer association', with the terms to be defined by the 2027 summit. Germany broke the silence procedure to force the change before the Montreal summit, with Italy and France also objecting.

3 November 2026

Canada will not be called an associate member of the European Union, because Germany said no. Berlin broke the silence procedure on 27 October, called the term a red line and led the opposition, according to EU diplomats. The statement adopted on 28 October instead offers 'ever closer association', short of full membership.

In September 2026, von der Leyen first offered Canada 'associate membership' in her State of the Union speech in Strasbourg, and EU diplomats say she did so without broadly consulting member states. The move surprised EU capitals. Germany, according to diplomats, argues the term 'member' complicates enlargement talks with countries such as Ukraine. In the diplomats' view, the proposal uses the term 'member' for a looser relationship while the EU negotiates full accession with Kyiv.

At least four countries broke the silence procedure before midday on 27 October, according to Politico, several of them refusing to sign a statement that contained the phrase 'associate membership' 48 hours before the Montreal summit. Chancellor Merz and other heads of state and government forced the term out of the draft final declaration, and besides Germany, Italy and France objected to the original text, Brussels diplomats told dpa, as reported by Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung and Rapporteur.

Germany's motive was partly its own record. According to diplomats and Commission officials, Merz had first floated the associate membership formula for Ukraine and considered it his own idea. Germany instead secured wording that obliges the EU and Canada to define an ever closer association, short of full membership, by the 2027 summit.

Carney played down the dropped term. He said the specific name does not matter, and what matters is the difference it makes to Canadians' lives and Canada becoming a more independent, more sovereign nation. Carney said the aim is to conclude negotiations by June 2027; the full substance, scale and scope would be determined in the coming months. Trump called the idea ridiculous and threatened high tariffs, or ending trade with Europe on many things, if it harmed US interests.
