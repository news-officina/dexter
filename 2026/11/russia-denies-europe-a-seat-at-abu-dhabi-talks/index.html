Russia denies Europe a seat at Abu Dhabi talks set by Washington without it

According to Politico and Euronews, the EU will send officials to parts of the negotiations, but Moscow says no agreement allows it, and the format was agreed by Russia, Ukraine and the United States alone. Kaja Kallas has said the EU will not implement any deal it is not party to.

9 November 2026

Russia says no agreement exists for European states, including France, to join the Abu Dhabi talks. Grigory Karasin, first deputy chairman of the Federation Council's international affairs committee, called Paris's stated plans wishful thinking and said agreement on such questions must come first. Ursula von der Leyen said the EU will be present alongside its partners.

Steve Witkoff announced the format himself on 3 November: trilateral talks among Russia, Ukraine and the United States in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November. According to Politico and Euronews, the European Commission and the European Council will send a joint team of senior officials; Russia disputes any European participation. The format was set without Europe, and Europe's seat rests on a disputed claim before the talks begin.

A senior EU diplomat told the Kyiv Independent that the national security advisers of Germany, France and the UK, plus chiefs of staff to the European Commission president and the Nato secretary-general, are set to take part in sections of the Abu Dhabi talks. Kaja Kallas said any agreement the EU is not party to, it will not implement. Radosław Sikorski called a deal over Europe's head impossible and demanded a ceasefire before European security is discussed.

Dmitry Peskov, the Russian presidential press secretary, said the talks in the coming days would be strictly trilateral, between Russia and Ukraine with the United States as mediator. Steve Witkoff said the discussions would cover post-war security arrangements, de-escalation, humanitarian efforts and next steps toward peace.

European officials believe the discussion of Europe's post-war security architecture is premature and misguided, given that Russia is yet to make a single concession at the negotiating table. António Costa, the European Council president, has agreed to put EU representation in the peace talks on the agenda of Friday's informal leaders' meeting in Dublin.
