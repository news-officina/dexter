Ukraine takes initiative on three fronts, ISW says, as Russian October losses hit record

Kyiv says more than 54,000 Russian soldiers were killed or seriously wounded in October, the highest toll since the 2022 invasion began. ISW says Ukraine seized the initiative in three directions and defeated Russia's northern pincer plan.

2 November 2026

The Institute for the Study of War (ISW) assesses Russia has lost the initiative across much of the theater, though not near Dobropillya.

Zelensky said more than 54,000 Russian soldiers were killed or seriously wounded in October, the highest human toll since the February 2022 invasion, and European government evaluations point in the same direction, Le Grand Continent reported. According to ISW, Russia gained ground in October only in Sumy and Zaporizhia oblasts and lost territory elsewhere, including Donetsk Oblast, its priority objective.

According to the Institute for the Study of War, Ukrainian forces have seized the initiative in the Kupyansk, Lyman and Oleksandrivka directions, and are contesting it in western Zaporizhia Oblast. In the Lyman direction, this means one Russian plan has failed outright. ISW and Al Jazeera report that Ukraine's Operation Vivaldi defeated Moscow's attempt to develop the northern pincer of a planned wide encirclement of the Fortress Belt.

Le Grand Continent writes that the cost falls hardest in Donetsk, where Russia concentrates its main offensive effort. It reports Russian forces lost about 60 km² east of Lyman in Luhansk oblast and along its border with Donetsk. It reports a further loss, 41.6 km², within the Donetsk region. According to Mashovets, cited by ISW, persistent Ukrainian counterattacks in the Oleksandrivka direction forced the Russian command to redeploy elements of the 120th Naval Infantry Division, the 40th Separate Naval Infantry Brigade, and the 90th Tank Division there. Those units would otherwise have supported offensives toward Druzhkivka or in the Hulyaipole direction.

Moscow scaled back offensive actions in the Kramatorsk, Oleksandrivka and Prydniprovske sectors, the Ukrainian General Staff reported on November 2, with no assaults recorded over the past day. Le Grand Continent counts roughly 300 square kilometres changing hands in October, gains and losses combined, along a front of more than 1,000 kilometres.
