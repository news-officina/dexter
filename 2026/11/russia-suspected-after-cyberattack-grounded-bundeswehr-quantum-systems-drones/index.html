Russia suspected after cyberattack grounded Bundeswehr Quantum Systems drones

German forces briefly suspended flights of Quantum Systems' Vector drones amid an investigation into hostile actors, with the BfV having warned of risks to firms supporting Ukraine.

13 November 2026

Germany's Bundeswehr briefly grounded its fleet of Quantum Systems drones after an internal investigation found an active cyberattack by hostile actors. A source close to the matter said initial indications pointed to Russia, and intelligence agencies are investigating. Quantum Systems said the fleet is flying again, in Germany and worldwide.

Quantum Systems said European defence firms supporting Ukraine are targeted precisely because their work is critically relevant to customers' security. In July, the BfV warned of a higher risk of Russian cyber attacks, espionage and sabotage. It named Germany's defence sector, and companies supporting Ukraine in particular. Qbase software plans flights for Vector reconnaissance drones supplied to Ukraine's Defence Forces and to the Bundeswehr, where they are known as the Falke.

According to Der Spiegel, it had already spread to the servers of the Bundeswehr, and to those of other customers. Quantum Systems declined to give further details.

German drone manufacturers have been observing increased attacks on their IT systems for some time. According to the FT, the Bundeswehr's suspension also kept Quantum Systems out of a recent military exercise.

Days before the attack was reported, the Bundeswehr reportedly said it intends to phase out Chinese-made drones, including DJI products, and to replace them with German or other European alternatives. Quantum Systems said the threat was contained thanks to the swift response of its IT and security teams in close cooperation with the authorities.
