Sport court orders ice hockey federation to readmit Russia for 2027 worlds

The Court of Arbitration for Sport found only substantiated security concerns can exclude athletes by nationality. Finland says Russian athletes will have no business coming to Finland, and the EU is weighing an entry ban for the Germany tournament.

2 November 2026

The Court of Arbitration for Sport has ordered the International Ice Hockey Federation to readmit Russian teams, including at the 2027 Men's World Championship in Germany. The 30 October 2026 ruling upheld one of three appeals by the Russian Ice Hockey Federation. The panel found the IIHF's safety and security arguments unsupported by sufficient evidence. Only substantiated security concerns, it concluded unanimously, can outweigh athletes of a particular nationality participating.

Finnish Foreign Minister Elina Valtonen said on 1 November that Russian athletes will have no business coming to Finland. She said she will advance the necessary measures in the coming days. Roberts Plavejs of the Latvian federation told LSM, the Latvian public broadcaster, that its sports law bars national teams from playing Russia or Belarus. The EU is weighing an entry ban for Germany 2027; Germany has not commented.

The IIHF said it is disappointed by the ruling but will comply.

It said its exclusion decisions were made to protect the safety of all participants and safeguard the integrity of its competitions.

The federation warned that Russia's return would affect tournament arrangements, schedules, travel and security.

In the IIHF's words, the ruling does not consider what cannot be implemented immediately given competitions already at an advanced stage of preparation.

The IIHF said it will urgently assess the steps and time needed to give effect to the ruling.

Anders Larsson said the IIHF had agreed that Russia, whenever it returns, comes back at the level it left: the top group for men's ice hockey. "the day they (Russia) return, whenever it is, they will return to the level they were at." Larsson also said Ukraine was the last team to qualify up, which could mean Ukraine is pushed out of the World Cup. The 30 October ruling covers IIHF championships including the 2027 Men's and Women's World Championships, the World Junior Championship and the U18 Championships. A two-day IIHF board meeting with Russia as its main topic began on Monday 2 November.

The appeal was heard in person in Lausanne on 23 and 26 October. Heikki Hietanen of the Finnish federation said Finland will not play against Russia as long as the war of aggression against Ukraine continues.
