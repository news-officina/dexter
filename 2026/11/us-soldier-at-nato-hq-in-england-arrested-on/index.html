US soldier at Nato HQ in England arrested on suspicion of spying for Russia

The 41-year-old, detained near Gloucester under the National Security Act 2023, was posted at Imjin Barracks, home of Nato's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps. He faces possible charges from both British authorities and the US Army, which can carry life imprisonment.

11 November 2026

A serving US Army soldier was arrested in England on suspicion of spying for Russia. Counter Terrorism Policing detained the 41-year-old near Gloucester on 10 November 2026 under the National Security Act 2023, in a joint investigation with US authorities, the US Military and the FBI.

The soldier was based at Imjin Barracks in Innsworth, Gloucestershire, headquarters of Nato's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps, the BBC understands. A source familiar with the situation said the man faces charges from both British authorities and the US Army. The specific charges are unclear, but US military court charges for espionage can carry life imprisonment. He remains in custody at a London police station.

Deputy Assistant Commissioner Vicki Evans of Counter Terrorism Policing London called the arrest significant. The allegations are serious and the investigation is very much ongoing. FBI director Kash Patel said in a statement that the arrest involves allegations of espionage of the utmost concern to the United States and its allies.

Searches are under way at two locations in Gloucestershire as part of the investigation, Counter Terrorism Policing said. The force stated that the case is not linked to any other ongoing Counter Terrorism Policing investigations.

The soldier's name is expected to be released in the coming days, once British authorities formally charge him, ABC News reported. The arrest was not connected with a suspected Iran-linked plot at RAF Fairford in September 2026.
