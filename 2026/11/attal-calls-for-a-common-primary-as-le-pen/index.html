Attal calls for a common primary as Le Pen leads the 2027 run-off polls

Ipsos puts Le Pen on 33.5% with Macron's 2022 base split across three candidates. Retailleau has rejected the idea, and Lisnard warns France must decide quickly.

2 November 2026

Gabriel Attal has called for a grande primaire of democratic parties to stop a 2027 run-off between Marine Le Pen and Jean-Luc Mélenchon. David Lisnard, who has proposed an open primary for two years, backed Attal's call.

The Toluna Harris Interactive poll for M6 and RTL sets Bruno Retailleau at 7% and Raphaël Glucksmann at 12% of first-round voting intentions for 2027. The poll was conducted online from October 19 to 21, 2026, among 2,094 registered voters.

In an Ipsos poll from October 2026, with Édouard Philippe as the sole candidate of Renaissance, MoDem and Horizons, Le Pen led at 33.5%. Philippe took 15.5% and Mélenchon 14%. Consolidation of Macron's 2022 base is equally uncertain: in the same poll, 42% of those voters would back Philippe, 18% Glucksmann and 13% Attal.

Bruno Retailleau rejected the primary outright, saying a primary that includes Raphaël Glucksmann makes no sense and plays into Marine Le Pen's hands. Lisnard urged speed, saying France must hurry because soon it will be too late.

Ipsos polled 1,500 French registered voters for Le Parisien on October 21 and 22, 2026, fielding voting intentions for the 2027 presidential election. Compared with the September Le Parisien wave (Attal 14.5%, Le Pen 34%), the October figures show Attal up and Le Pen slightly down.
