Coalition of the Willing holds first exercise in Poland from 9 to 22 November

RMF FM reports nearly 5,000 soldiers taking part, joined by British, French and limited German forces, to test the command and logistics systems needed to move troops across Europe. The United States is not taking part, and in July Russia's ambassador called the drills symbolic.

2 November 2026

The Coalition of the Willing will hold its Koalicja exercise at Nowa Dęba in Poland from 9 to 22 November 2026. RMF FM reported almost 5,000 soldiers taking part, nearly 2,000 of them Polish. Le Monde reported almost 3,000; a defence ministry lieutenant colonel said that figure covers only foreign soldiers.

The exercise is the coalition's first. Its leaders decided in July 2026 to hold it in Poland in autumn 2026, with British and French forces taking part. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk announced then that the manoeuvres would strengthen allied readiness to act for the region's security and Ukraine's.

Germany's Bundeswehr joins the exercise with about ten staff officers, tanker aircraft and logistics, and operations planned over Romania and the western Black Sea. The German defence ministry confirmed the deployment. Le Monde reported that the manoeuvres are not a show of force with tanks and rockets. Their purpose is to test the command system and the logistics procedures for moving troops and equipment across Europe. Counter-drone work is a key element of the exercises.

In July 2026, Russian Ambassador Andrey Kelin told the Russian daily Izvestia that deployment of foreign troops and bases on Ukrainian territory is completely unacceptable. The TASS news agency carried his remarks. Kelin also dismissed the planned exercises in Poland as a symbolic show of resolve and unity rather than a large-scale military activity. He said he did not think the upcoming military gathering in Poland would turn into anything large-scale.

The United States is not taking part in the exercise, Ukrainian media reported. Its participation in future security guarantees for Ukraine remains largely uncertain.
