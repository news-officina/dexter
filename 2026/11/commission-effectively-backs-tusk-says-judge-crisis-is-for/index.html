Commission effectively backs Tusk, says judge crisis is for Warsaw

The Commission says appointing Constitutional Tribunal judges lies with the Sejm, citing a European court order not to block the new judges. President Nawrocki denounced the takeover of the Tribunal and swore in only two of the six.

3 November 2026

The European Commission said appointing Constitutional Tribunal judges lies within the constitutional power of the Sejm, and it effectively backed Donald Tusk. "It is for the Polish authorities to ensure that the Constitutional Tribunal can function in accordance with the Polish constitution and in respect of the relevant case law of the Court of Justice of the European Union."

The European Commission cited an ECtHR interim order of 5 May 2026 requiring Polish authorities not to hinder the judges elected by the Sejm in March 2026 from taking up their duties. President Nawrocki denounced the events at the Tribunal as a violation of Poland's constitutional order and a forcible takeover of a constitutional organ. He called for government-sent coercive forces to leave and announced urgent consultations with parliamentary forces.

President Nawrocki swore in only two of the six judges elected by the Sejm, Magdalena Bentkowska and Dariusz Szostak, who took the oath in his presence. On 9 April the other four took the oath in the Sejm, swearing before the president. They filed written oath texts at the office of the president's chancellery. Tribunal president Święczkowski said he could not recognise that event.

The deadlock turned physical on the night of Friday 30 to Saturday 31 October 2026. President Nawrocki entered the Tribunal building amid the standoff over the new judges. He came into an exchange of words and shoving with a group of judges, as protesters gathered outside. That same day, 30 October, judge Anna Korwin-Piotrowska announced she was provisionally taking over the Tribunal's leadership. She suspended president Bogdan Święczkowski and vice-president Bartłomiej Sochański for a month. Both men contest the legality of her decisions.

On 2 November Donald Tusk called on President Nawrocki to recognise the Sejm-elected judges and let them start work. Justice minister and Prosecutor General Waldemar Żurek has requested that the immunity of the suspended Tribunal leaders be waived.
