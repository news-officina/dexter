Commission launches defence campaign amid 71% support for an EU army

Eurobarometer finds 81% back a common defence policy, but Globsec reports only 49% believe Europe could defend itself without the US. Support for an EU army reaches 82% in Romania.

14 November 2026

The European Commission launched a public campaign on 9 November 2026 called "European defence: protect what matters", to promote common security and defence. Its Eurobarometer figures show 81% of Europeans back a common defence policy among Member States. Yet only 49% believe Europe could secure itself without strong US support, Globsec reports.

GLOBSEC's 2026 polling found 71% of Europeans want the EU to have its own army. Globsec reports that 83% agree Europe should take on more responsibility if the US reduces its role. Whether it will be acted on is another matter: in April, Politico Europe reported that the EU's top diplomat, Kaja Kallas, had rejected the idea of an EU army.

The Commission's Readiness 2030 plan, launched in 2025, mobilises up to €800 billion to close capability gaps, strengthen industrial capacity, and accelerate investment in collective security. Its SAFE instrument has so far disbursed almost €13.5 billion across 11 Member States.

GLOBSEC's 2026 survey put an EU army at 82% in Romania, 78% in Lithuania and 75% in France. Max Bergmann, director of the Europe, Russia and Eurasia Programme at the Centre for Strategic and International Studies, said a European army makes sense in principle. The main stumbling block, he said, is that it is "not seen as practical". In February, Liana Fix, senior fellow for Europe at the Council on Foreign Relations, warned that decision-making and European command structures must be solved. She called these the most important military questions, and said so far everyone is still relying on NATO structures.

On 13 November, Andrius Kubilius, the European Commissioner for Defence and Space, wrote on Bluesky that Europeans want "more Europe" and "stronger Europe" inside of NATO for the defence of Europe. On the same day he shared a Politico article reporting that European citizens overwhelmingly back the creation of an EU army, according to a survey.
