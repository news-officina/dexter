Italy to offer Ukraine access to two military radar satellites

Defence Minister Guido Crosetto takes the thirteenth aid package to the Copasir committee on 10 November, as Washington limits its own satellite support to Kyiv.

10 November 2026

Italy will give Ukrainian forces access to two Cosmo-SkyMed radar satellites under its thirteenth military aid package. Defence Minister Guido Crosetto takes the package to the Copasir parliamentary committee on 10 November. Defending the decree mechanism, he said the Draghi government and practically all governing and opposition parties chose and voted for it. If it was right then, he said, it is hard to explain why it is unjust now.

The access matters because Washington has limited intelligence and satellite support to Ukraine, a decision Crosetto called a complete mistake in October. The data will be limited to Ukrainian territory, so Kyiv cannot use it to strike inside Russia.

The coalition itself is split. Salvini said he would propose a halt to arms and money for Ukraine. He would raise it in the Council of Ministers. Antonio Tajani, his fellow Deputy Prime Minister, answered that Italy stands with Ukraine and will help it for as long as necessary. The next military aid package, he said, is ready to go to Kyiv as soon as possible.

Crosetto told journalists on 6 November that under the decree mechanism Italy transfers equipment only, with no direct money going to Kyiv. The government had nonetheless prepared the thirteenth package, he said. It keeps a commitment made in the chamber as a government, and one it would continue to keep. Ukraine once stopped 90% of attacks but now stops 10%, Crosetto said. Russian technology has evolved, and the speed of the new Shahed drones makes them hard or impossible to stop.

According to an estimate by the Institute of International Affairs published in 2026, Italian military assistance to Ukraine has exceeded 3 billion euros. The government approved the twelfth package in November 2025, published in the Official Gazette on 1 December. Crosetto will submit the thirteenth to Copasir and propose extending the decree-based mechanism, which he noted was not initiated by the Meloni government, into 2027. The decision on prolonging the mechanism into 2027 now rests with the government's proposal.
